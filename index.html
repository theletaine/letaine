The staff always smells of smoke. And I’ve wandered to the engineer’s car to compare the scent. They’re not smelling of coal or diesel. It’s fully fledged darts. I’m of the opinion that those who wish to choke on cigarettes are free to do so, but to do so in an enclosed space means you have the manners of an ass. Would you like an ass to serve your drinks? Your meals? No? Wise of you. I’d agree. I’ve heard the conductor banned and labeled such items “employee contraband.” Yet he fails to keep the staff under his watch. The fact that the leader of such a machine is losing control over his gears and spokes means there’s much running amok within the chassis. Yet, I will reserve some praise for the impressive machinations of the engine. It runs smoothly, with barely any dark smoke visible from the circular panel of glass I’d viewed it through. This is just my second day aboard the Letaine. There is plenty to uncover. Follow my soon-to-be lengthy review to learn of the operations of this cross-continental train.
